An editorial front page summing up the world news of Wednesday 18 November 2026 in one image: drones over the Gulf as Iran strikes U.S. bases in Kuwait and Bahrain and the U.S. hits a missile site on Qeshm Island; the Kerch Bridge's road arch collapsing into the strait; the Quad meeting in Manila; Nvidia's record $115.8bn quarter; Cyclone Akvan in the Bay of Bengal; and Canada turning to China at APEC in Shenzhen. Every story was verified against reports dated 18 November 2026, and all artwork was drawn for this front page.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

This copies `entry.png` (1024×1024, under 1 MiB) to the output directory. Node 22, standard library only, no network.
